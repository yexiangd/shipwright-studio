Habit Tracker

A habit tracker is a free online tool that helps you keep small daily promises to yourself — add a habit, check it off once a day with a single tap, and watch the streak grow as the days stack up. Everything is stored privately in this browser's local storage; no account, no sign-up, nothing ever leaves your device.

Manage habits

  • How streaks work. A habit's streak counts consecutive days you've checked it in, ending today. If you haven't checked in yet today, yesterday's streak still shows — tap it today to keep it alive. One missed day resets the streak to zero; your best streak is never lost.
  • Private by design. Your habits live only in this browser's local storage. Clear your browser data and they disappear; nothing is sent anywhere.
