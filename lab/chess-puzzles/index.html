Chess Puzzles

Chess Puzzles is a free online tool that serves up mate-in-two chess tactics, one puzzle at a time — find the single key move that forces checkmate on the next move, whatever Black replies. Six puzzles, from a gentle warm-up to a quiet waiting move. No account, no clock, nothing leaves your browser.

Every puzzle on this page was machine-verified as a genuine, unique mate in two: there is no mate in one, exactly one key move works, and every legal Black reply is mated on move two. If a move looks like it should work and doesn't, that is the puzzle.

Find the key move. Click a white piece, then a highlighted square.

A clean streak counts consecutive puzzles solved without a single wrong move — a hint never breaks it, but a wrong move does. Progress is saved in this browser only (localStorage).

How it works

Mate in 2 means White can force checkmate on the second move, no matter what Black does. First find the key move — often a quiet move that creates an unstoppable threat. Black replies, and then you deliver mate. There is exactly one key move in each puzzle; every other legal move lets Black escape.

Click a piece to see where it can go (brass dots), then click a destination. After the key move, Black's reply plays automatically and you finish the job. Stuck? The hint shows only the square the key move starts from. The solution shows the whole verified line — but a puzzle finished that way doesn't count as solved.
