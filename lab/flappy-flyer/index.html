Flappy Flyer

Flappy Flyer is a free online game that lets you tap to flap a little brass bird through gaps in oncoming pipes — one point per pipe, with the gaps shrinking and the world speeding up as your score climbs. It runs entirely in your browser: no downloads, no accounts, no tracking.

How it works

Each tap sets the bird's velocity to a −420 px/s upward impulse; gravity (1500 px/s²) takes over the instant you let go. Every pipe you clear is +1 — but the gap narrows from 165 px to 112 px and the world speeds up from 190 to 300 px/s as your score climbs to 40. Rage responsibly.

Difficulty numbers

Gap and speed ramp linearly from score 0 to score 40, then hold:
score 0 → gap 165 px, speed 190 px/s · 10 → 151.75, 217.5 · 20 → 138.5, 245 · 30 → 125.25, 272.5 · 40+ → 112, 300.
Physics: gravity 1500 px/s², flap impulse −420 px/s, max fall 560 px/s, bird radius 14 px, pipe width 64 px, spawn every 1.5 s. Physics integrates in fixed 1/120 s substeps, so 60 Hz and 120 Hz displays produce identical trajectories.
