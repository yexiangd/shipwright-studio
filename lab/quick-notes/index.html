Quick Notes

Quick Notes is a free online tool that lets you jot down quick sticky notes in a browser-extension-style popup — create, pin, colour and search them, and they stay saved in your browser, even after you close the tab.

A demo of the browser-extension popup — notes are saved in this browser only, via localStorage. This page is not a real extension, and nothing syncs or leaves your device.
