Flexbox in 60 seconds


    

  • The main axis follows flex-direction (row = left to right). justify-content spreads items along it; align-items aligns them across it.
    • 

  • With wrap on, align-content spreads whole rows; with nowrap it does nothing — that is why it only matters once items wrap.
    • 

  • flex-grow lets an item take a share of leftover space; flex-basis is its starting size before growing or shrinking. align-self overrides align-items for one item only.
