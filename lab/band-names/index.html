      How it works

      Big word banks. Every name is assembled from banks of 48 universal adjectives and nouns, plus genre-specific banks — twelve adjectives and twelve nouns per genre — so repeats are rare and each genre genuinely sounds like itself. Metal leans Obsidian and Tormented; Jazz leans Velvety and Moonlit.

      Four naming patterns. "The + Noun", "Adjective + Noun", "The + Adjective + Noun" and "Noun + Noun" cover the shapes real band names take — or let Mix choose a pattern at random. A short tagline rides along with each name, because a band name deserves a little attitude.

      One name a day. The band name of the day is derived from the date with a seeded generator, so every visitor sees the same name on the same day — and a different one tomorrow.

      Nothing leaves your browser. Generation, favourites and history all run locally on this page. Favourites are stored in your browser's local storage; clearing them is one click.
