Lorem Studio

Lorem Studio is a free online tool that generates themed placeholder text — classic Latin lorem ipsum, startup buzzword copy, or pirate-speak — so your mockups sound like the finished product before a single real word is written. Pick a theme, choose how much text you need, and copy it out as plain text or HTML in one click.

Everything runs in your browser — no accounts, no uploads, no network calls. The text is generated from built-in sentence banks, so every click gives you a fresh variation.

Copy as HTML wraps each paragraph in <p>…</p> tags, ready to paste into a markup file. The download is named after the theme, e.g. lorem-studio-startup.txt.

How it works

Each theme has its own bank of sentences, written in that voice — traditional Latin for Classic, boardroom-ready buzzwords for Startup, and full pirate dialect for Pirate. Every time you press Generate, the bank is shuffled with a fresh seed and sentences are dealt out into paragraphs, so repeated clicks give different text and no two paragraphs in one output are ever identical. Short paragraphs hold 2–3 sentences, medium 4–5, and long 6–8.

Placeholder text is a stand-in, not a message — swap it for real copy before anything goes live.
