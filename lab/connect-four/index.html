Connect Four vs AI

Connect Four vs AI is a free online tool that lets you drop discs into a classic 7×6 grid and try to line up four in a row before a minimax computer opponent does. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard, choose who opens, or hand the device to a friend for local 2-player — winning lines light up, moves undo cleanly, and your lifetime record is kept between visits.
