Bridge Fee Comparator

Bridge Fee Comparator is a free online tool that compares cross-chain bridge costs side by side, so you can see which bridge is cheapest and which is fastest for your route before you move any funds. Pick a source chain, a destination chain, and an amount — the ranked comparison updates instantly, with a fee breakdown, estimated time, and total cost for each bridge.

All fees and times are illustrative demo estimates for comparison — not live prices. Real bridge costs change with gas, liquidity, and market conditions. No wallet connection is needed; nothing leaves this page.

    How fees are calculated

    Each bridge charges a flat fee plus a percentage of the amount bridged: fee = flat fee + amount × percentage. Total cost is the fee; you receive the amount minus the fee. Times are the bridge's typical range from submission to funds arriving on the destination chain.
