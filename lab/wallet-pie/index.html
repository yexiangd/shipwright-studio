Wallet Portfolio Pie

Wallet Portfolio Pie is a free online tool that turns manually entered holdings into a donut chart of portfolio allocation, so you can see at a glance how your money is spread across stocks, funds, crypto, cash and anything else you type in. Add each holding by name and value, and the chart, weights and totals update instantly — nothing is fetched from the internet and nothing leaves your browser.

All values here are manually entered demo data — not live prices. Prices change constantly; this page never fetches market data, so the numbers you see are exactly the numbers you typed in.

      How it works

      Each holding's value is either quantity × price per unit, or the direct value you typed in. Its weight is its value divided by the total portfolio value: weight = value ÷ total. The donut chart draws one slice per holding, sized by weight, and the table sorts holdings by value, largest first.

      This is a planning and visualisation aid, not investment advice. Values are entered by hand and are never checked against live markets — double-check any number that matters before acting on it.
