      How it would work

      • Blocklist. Pick the sites that pull you away — social feeds, video, news — or add any domain of your own. The real extension would intercept those tabs the moment a session starts.
      • Timer. A session is a commitment: 25, 50, or 90 minutes of protected time. Pausing is allowed; quitting early breaks the streak.
      • Blocked screen. Slip and click a blocked site mid-session and you meet a wall instead of the feed — plus a tally of every attempt you survived. Stats live in localStorage only, on this browser, and reset clears them.
