    How gas works

    Every blockchain transaction pays for computation in units called gas. The gas price — usually quoted in gwei, a billionth of the native token — is what you pay per unit, so total fee = gas used × gas price. Since the London upgrade, Ethereum-style chains split the price into a base fee set by the network and a priority fee (tip) you add to jump the queue. Layer-2 chains batch many transactions together, which is why their gas prices look tiny next to Ethereum's. The numbers on this page are fixed demo values with a small simulated random walk — they illustrate the mechanics, not today's market.
