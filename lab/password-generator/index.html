Why local generation matters: a password is only as private as the machine that creates it. Forge uses your browser's cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) and never transmits anything — you can even disconnect from the internet after this page loads. Rule of thumb: use random passwords for accounts (a password manager remembers them) and passphrases for the few secrets you must type yourself. 100+ bits of entropy is overkill for anything outside nation-state territory.
