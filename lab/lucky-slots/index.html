How the odds work

  • Each reel is an independent weighted draw: cherry 28%, lemon 22%, bell 18%, star 14%, diamond 10%, seven 8%. Nothing is rigged toward or against you — the weights are printed above.
  • Only the centre line pays. Wins are computed straight from the paytable, so you can check any spin yourself.
  • Credits and stats live in localStorage only. Resetting credits gives you a fresh 100 and clears the history; your lifetime stats stay until you reset them.
