Press Space to start or stop. Sound starts only after you press Start or Space, because browsers block audio autoplay.

How does it keep time?

Tap tempo takes the median interval between your recent taps, so one rushed or dragging tap does not skew the tempo. Once running, a lookahead scheduler checks every 25 ms and queues each click against the audio clock 0.1 seconds ahead, so the beat does not drift the way a plain timer would. BPM counts the numerator beats in each bar, so 6/8 gives six beats per bar with beat 1 accented.
