Joke Machine

Joke Machine is a free online tool that deals a random programmer joke — setup first, punchline on reveal — and lets you rate it on a groan scale, with no repeats until the deck runs out and session stats for the best and worst jokes. No account, nothing leaves the page.

How it works

  • The groan scale — 😂 is a 1 (no groan, actually funny), 😐 is a 2, 🙄 is a 3 (maximum groan). Lower average groan means funnier. A joke rated once already counts towards best and worst.
  • The no-repeat deck — jokes are shuffled into a deck and dealt one at a time. You will not see the same joke twice until the deck is empty, then it reshuffles and deals again.
  • Everything runs in your browser — the 32 jokes live in this page, and your stats are saved on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
