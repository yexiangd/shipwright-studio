    How it works

    A polite request, not a lock. A robots.txt file tells well-behaved crawlers — search engines, AI bots, archivers — which parts of your site they may visit. It does not block bad actors, and it is no substitute for authentication on private pages. Place the file at the root of your site, at /robots.txt.

    Groups, then rules. Each group names a crawler (or * for all of them) and lists the paths it may or may not visit. Groups are printed in order, separated by a blank line, followed by the optional crawl-delay and sitemap lines. The builder checks each rule as you type — empty paths, duplicates, stray wildcards and bad sitemap URLs are flagged, never silently fixed.

    Your policy stays yours. Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or tracked. Copy the file, download it as robots.txt, and upload it to your site's root.
