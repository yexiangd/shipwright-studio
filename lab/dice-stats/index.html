How the maths works

  • The exact curve is computed by convolution: the distribution of one die is combined with itself once per extra die, so every total's probability is exact — no simulation involved. Drop-lowest, advantage and disadvantage are computed by enumerating every possible combination instead.
  • That is why 2d6 clusters at 7: there are six ways to make 7 and only one way to make 2 or 12. A single d20 is flat — every face is equally likely.
  • Advantage (keep the higher of 2d20) lifts the average roll from 10.5 to about 13.8; disadvantage drops it to about 7.2. The pale bars are your observed rolls — roll enough and they settle onto the brass curve.
  • Everything is stored in localStorage in your browser only. If storage is unavailable the page still works, it just forgets the session when you leave.
