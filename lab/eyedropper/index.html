Screen Picker

Screen Picker is a free online tool that samples any color on your screen and converts it to HEX, RGB, HSL and CSS variables. Use the browser eyedropper to grab a color from anywhere on the page, or type a hex by hand — then click any format to copy it. No download, no account, nothing leaves your browser.

How it works

The eyedropper. In Chrome or Edge, click "Pick a color from your screen" and your cursor becomes a magnifier — click anywhere, even outside this page, and the color lands here. The API is Chromium-only, so Safari and Firefox users get the manual picker instead; the conversions and copying work identically either way.

Every format, one click. Each row copies its exact value — #D2A24C, rgb(210, 162, 76), hsl(39, 60%, 56%), or a ready-to-paste --color variable — so the color goes straight into your stylesheet, your design file, or your chat with a developer.

Yours, on your device. Recently picked colors are stored in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere. Clear them any time.
