Focus Timer

Focus Timer is a free online Pomodoro timer that runs the classic 25-minute focus / 5-minute break rhythm, auto-serves a long break every four sessions, and tracks today's completed pomodoros. The countdown lives in your browser tab, so it keeps you honest while you work.

How it works: one pomodoro is 25 minutes of single-task focus followed by a 5-minute break; after four, take a 15–30 minute long break. The technique (named for a tomato-shaped kitchen timer) works because the short horizon makes starting cheap and the breaks make stopping mandatory. This page keeps everything in localStorage on your device — your session counts never leave it.
