Date Night Generator

Date Night Generator is a free online tool that builds a complete date night plan in seconds. Pick a vibe, a budget and how much time you have, and it assembles a full evening — a pre-dinner activity, dinner and an after-dinner wind-down — with timings that actually add up. Swap any step, shuffle the whole night, or let Surprise Me decide everything.

    How it works

    1. Choose your settings. A vibe sets the mood, a budget band keeps suggestions honest, and the time you have decides how long each step can run.

    2. Get a three-step evening. Every plan runs pre-dinner → dinner → after-dinner, with start and end times that fit your window — no maths required.

    3. Make it yours. Swap any single step, shuffle the whole night, or hit Surprise me and go with whatever comes up. Copy the plan to share it with your date.
