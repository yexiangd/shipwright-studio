      How it works

      Every pitch is built from five slots: an audience with a problem, solved with a technology, sold as a business model, and finished with a twist. The name is coined from the roll, the tagline is picked from a few templates, and the pitch paragraph stitches the five slots into one sentence each. Lock any slot to hold it steady while the rest re-roll — lock all five and Generate changes nothing at all.

      The Meta button fixes the slots to the idea for this very tool: aspiring founders, running out of startup ideas, solved by a random idea generator. Some ideas are jokes. Some jokes are fundable. Tell them apart at your own risk.
