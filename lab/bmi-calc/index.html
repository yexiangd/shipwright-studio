What BMI can't tell you

BMI is a ratio of weight to height — it can't tell muscle from fat, so a very muscular person can score high while being lean. It says nothing about where fat sits on the body, and it doesn't account for age, sex, or ethnicity. Use it as one rough signal among many, not as a diagnosis. If the number worries you, a clinician — not a website — is the right next step.
