Wordle Clone

A Wordle clone is a free online word game that gives you six guesses to find a secret five-letter word — after every guess, each tile turns green, gold or grey to show which letters are correct, misplaced or absent. Play the daily puzzle (one word for everyone, every day) or practise on unlimited random words. Everything runs locally in your browser.
