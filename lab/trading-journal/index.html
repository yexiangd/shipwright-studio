How are the stats calculated?

Win rate is winning trades divided by decided trades (wins plus losses; break-even trades count in neither). Profit factor is gross profit divided by gross loss — above 1 means winners outweigh losers. Expectancy is average P&L per trade: what one trade is worth on average across the whole journal. P&L here is gross per-trade profit — no commissions, fees, or taxes are included.

This is a personal journal tool, not financial advice. Figures are gross P&L only and exclude commissions, fees, and taxes.
