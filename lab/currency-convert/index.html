How conversion works

The formula

Every rate is stored as units per 1 US dollar. To convert, the amount is first turned into dollars, then into the target currency: result = amount ÷ rate(from) × rate(to). The reverse rate is simply the reciprocal, so converting back returns the starting amount (within rounding).

Rounding

Most currencies show two decimals. Yen and won have no minor unit, so they show whole numbers. Rates are shown to four significant figures where it helps readability.

Why sample rates?

Real exchange rates move every second and need a market data feed. This tool is a fast estimator, not a trading desk — so it uses fixed, dated sample rates and says so plainly, rather than pretending to be live. For a real transaction, always check your bank or card provider's rate.
