Sharpe Calculator

Sharpe Calculator is a free online tool that computes risk-adjusted return ratios from a pasted series of monthly returns or prices — annualised Sharpe and Sortino, max drawdown, volatility, CAGR and win rate — and charts the equity curve with drawdowns shaded. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Your returns series

Numbers with |x| ≥ 1 are read as %; the rest as decimals.
Used as the benchmark for Sharpe & Sortino (monthly = annual ÷ 12).

Risk-adjusted metrics

Paste a returns series above, or load the sample data, to compute the metrics.
—
Sharpe (ann.)
Excess return per unit of total volatility. >1 is good, >2 is excellent.
—
Sortino (ann.)
Like Sharpe but only downside volatility counts — upside swings aren't punished.
—
Max drawdown
Largest peak-to-trough fall of the equity curve. The worst ride you'd have sat through.
—
Volatility (ann.)
Annualised standard deviation of monthly returns — how bumpy the ride was.
—
CAGR
Compound annual growth rate from the monthly series. Return you actually kept.
—
Win rate
Share of months with a positive return. Frequency, not magnitude.

  • Sharpe punishes upside volatility too. If the strategy occasionally moons, Sortino is the fairer read.
  • Ratios are estimates from the past, not promises about the future.
