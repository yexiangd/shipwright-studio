How it works

Bitcoin halves its block reward every 210,000 blocks — roughly once every four years. Miners currently earn 3.125 BTC per block; at block 1,050,000 that drops to 1.5625 BTC, and new daily issuance falls from about 450 to about 225 BTC.

The date is an estimate, not a schedule. The protocol targets one block every 10 minutes, but real blocks arrive faster or slower, and difficulty only retargets every 2,016 blocks. The countdown above assumes exactly 10 minutes per block, so the true halving date can land days either side of the estimate.

Nothing here is live data. This page makes no network requests to any chain or price service. If you know the real current height from a block explorer, enter it above and the countdown, progress bar and date will recompute instantly.
