How it works

A word is a run of characters separated by spaces. A sentence ends at a full stop, exclamation mark or question mark; a final fragment without a terminator still counts as one sentence. Paragraphs are blocks of text separated by blank lines. Reading time assumes 200 words per minute and speaking time 130 words per minute. Keyword density counts each word as a share of all words; words shorter than three letters and, by default, common stopwords like "the" and "and" are left out of the table.
