Pocket Piano

Pocket Piano is a free online tool that plays a two-octave piano right in your browser — press the keys with mouse, touch, or your computer keyboard, hold notes with sustain, and switch voices, with nothing to download and nothing leaving your browser.

How to play

Click or tap the keys, or type on your computer keyboard: A=C, W=C#, S=D, E=D#, D=E, F=F, T=F#, G=G, Y=G#, H=A, U=A#, J=B, K=C (next octave), O=C#, L=D, P=D#, ;=E (next octave). The keyboard map follows the current base octave, so Octave up moves every typed note up with the displayed keys. Sustain Off stops a note about 0.15 seconds after you lift; Sustain On lets it ring for about 1.5 seconds, so chords overlap. The demo tune plays the opening of "Ode to Joy" in the current base octave.
