    Grid in 60 seconds
    

      

    • A track is one column or row. grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr) makes four equal tracks; gap is the gutter between them.
      • 

    • Items sit on lines, not cells: grid-column: 1 / 3 starts at line 1 and ends at line 3, so it spans two columns. Lines are numbered from 1, and the last line is tracks + 1.
      • 

    • grid-row works the same way down the page. Give each item a column span and a row span and the layout is fully described — no floats, no wrappers.
