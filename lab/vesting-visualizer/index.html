How it works


    

  • TGE unlock frees a slice on day one. Cliff means nothing further unlocks until that month passes. After the cliff, the rest unlocks in equal monthly slices over the vesting period.
    • 

  • Why the curve is flat, then straight: flat during the cliff, then a straight line while equal slices vest, then flat again at 100%.
    • 

  • Rounding: monthly slices are rounded for display; the final month absorbs any remainder so the total is exact.
