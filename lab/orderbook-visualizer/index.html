    How to read an order book

    • Bid — the highest price buyers are willing to pay. Ask — the lowest price sellers will accept. The best bid and best ask sit on either side of the mid-price line.
    • Spread — best ask minus best bid. It is the cost of demanding immediacy; a tight spread means a liquid market, a wide one means thin trading.
    • Depth — resting size at each price. The depth chart stacks it cumulatively: the higher the curve at the middle, the more size is waiting near the touch.
    • Limit order — "I will buy/sell at this price or better." It rests in the book and adds depth. Market order — "fill me now." It takes depth, walking through price levels and pushing the mid-price.
    • Cancel — a resting order is withdrawn, thinning the book. Watch the feed: makers stepping inside a wide spread tighten it again.
    • Mid-price — the average of best bid and best ask. A reference point, not a tradable price.

    Every number on this page is generated by a local random simulation for education. It is not market data, not a quote, and not financial advice.
