How the bot works

  • The bot is openly "suspiciously lucky": 60% of the time it plays the move that beats your most-played move so far; the other 40% it picks at random.
  • Ties in most-played count are broken in the order rock, then paper, then scissors.
  • Nothing leaves your browser — stats live in localStorage only.
