How it works

Everything is removed except the words. The extractor deletes scripts, styles, navigation, sidebars, headers, footers and forms, plus anything labelled as an ad, pop-up, cookie banner, newsletter box or share bar — then keeps headings, paragraphs, lists and blockquotes, in their original order.

Reading time assumes 200 words per minute, a standard average for adult readers. Word count is the number of words in the cleaned text, so the estimate reflects what you actually read, not the clutter around it.

Nothing leaves your browser. The samples, the extraction and the typography controls all run locally on this page. No article text is sent to any server, and no URL fetching is possible from a static page — that is why paste-your-own takes raw HTML instead of a link.
