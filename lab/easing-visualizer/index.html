How it works

  • P1 and P2 are the two control points of a cubic-bezier curve. Their x positions set when the motion accelerates; their y positions set how far it has travelled.
  • Y may exceed 0–1. A y above 1 makes the animation overshoot its target and settle back — that is what gives ease-out-back its springy feel.
  • Bounce is not a cubic-bezier. A true bounce needs several direction changes, which one bezier curve cannot express — use keyframes for that.
