It also estimates what a transaction costs at any gas price. Everything runs in your browser; nothing leaves the page.

How the units relate

  • Wei is the smallest unit of ether: 1 ether = 10¹⁸ wei, and 1 gwei = 10⁹ wei. On-chain, every amount is an integer number of wei — there is no such thing as half a wei.
  • That is why this page converts with BigInt integer math instead of floating point: a JavaScript Number can only represent integers exactly up to 2⁵³, while 1 ether is already 10¹⁸ wei. Floats would silently round your money away.
  • Gas is priced in gwei and paid in wei: cost = gas limit × gas price. A standard transfer uses 21,000 gas, so at 20 gwei it costs 420,000 gwei — 0.00042 ether.
