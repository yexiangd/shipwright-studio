How to use it

Flip. Each card shows a term first — gas, MEV, rollup, DAO. Click it (or press Space) to see a plain-English definition, with no jargon assumed.

Sort. If the definition clicks, press I know this. If it is still fuzzy, press Still learning — the card goes into a pile you will see again. Your streak counts consecutive Knows.

Review. When the deck is done, hit Review still-learning to re-deal only the cards you missed. Filter by category to drill one area — Basics, DeFi, Infrastructure or Security — and shuffle whenever you want a fresh order.

Nothing here is financial advice. These cards explain words, not investments. No wallet, no account, no data leaves your browser.
