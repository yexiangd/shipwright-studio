Orb Eater

Orb Eater is a free online game that drops you into a petri dish arena. Steer your orb with mouse, touch or keyboard, eat pellets and any orb smaller than you to grow, and dodge anything bigger — one touch from a larger orb ends your run. No download, no account: your best score is saved on your device.

How to play

The one rule. You can eat anything at least 10% smaller than you; anything at least 10% bigger can eat you. Mass is conserved by area, so a meal makes you visibly larger — and larger orbs move slower, so every meal is a trade-off between size and speed. You also shed a little mass every second, so standing still slowly shrinks you.

The other orbs play by the same rules. Enemies chase pellets and anything smaller than them, flee from anything bigger, and eat each other when they get the chance. Watch the crowd: a feeding frenzy nearby means a giant is about to appear.

Make it yours. Chill slows the arena down, Spicy makes enemies faster, bolder and bigger from the start — the choice applies to your next run. Your best score is saved in your browser, and nothing about your game ever leaves this page.
