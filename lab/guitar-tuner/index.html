How it works

The needle

The microphone signal is analysed with the YIN pitch-detection algorithm to find the pitch, then compared with the target note. The difference is measured in cents — one hundredth of a semitone — using cents = 1200 × log₂(f / f_target). The needle swings from −50 to +50 cents; the green band in the middle is ±5 cents, which is close enough that the string counts as in tune.

Standard tuning

From the thickest string to the thinnest: E2 82.41 Hz, A2 110.00 Hz, D3 146.83 Hz, G3 196.00 Hz, B3 246.94 Hz, E4 329.63 Hz. In chromatic mode the tuner names whatever note it hears, so it works for other instruments too.

Privacy

Audio is analysed live in your browser and never leaves your device. The tuner only starts listening when you press Start, and stops completely when you press Stop.
