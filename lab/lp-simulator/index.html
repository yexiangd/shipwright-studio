What the range does — and the model assumptions

  • Narrow range: you earn a bigger share of fees while the price is inside it — but the price spends more days outside it, earning nothing and sitting in a single token.
  • Wide range: you stay in range longer and keep earning, but your share of each day's fees is smaller.
  • Fees model: on each in-range day you earn fee tier × daily volume × (capital ÷ pool TVL). Out-of-range days earn zero. This is a teaching model: real pools split fees by active liquidity and ticks, and charge gas.
  • Price path: a seeded daily random walk (same inputs → same path). It is a scenario, not a forecast. Impermanent loss is measured against holding the original token amounts, before fees.
