Excuse Generator

Excuse Generator is a free online tool that writes a believable excuse for a missed deadline, a late arrival, a missed call, or a reply that's days overdue. Pick the situation, set the tone, choose how bold you're feeling — and get a ready-to-send excuse, with a plausibility meter so you know exactly how far you're stretching it.

Every excuse here is written to be plausible, not provable — use the bold ones at your own risk.

How it works

  1. Pick your situation — the scenario, the tone, and how bold you're willing to be.
  2. Generate — every excuse is drawn from a hand-written library, and none repeats until the whole pool has been shown.
  3. Copy and send — check the plausibility meter first. Five dots means nobody will blink; one dot means you should probably send it from a different time zone.
