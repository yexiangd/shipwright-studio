How to play

  • Run and jump — hold a direction and jump. Hold jump a little longer to jump a little higher.
  • Wall-slide — while falling next to a wall, hold toward it to slide down slowly instead of dropping.
  • Wall-jump — press jump while sliding to kick off the wall. Chain slides and jumps to climb shafts.
  • Coins and gems — coins line the natural path; each level hides one gem somewhere riskier. Spikes and pits send you back to the start.
  • Best times — the timer starts on your first move and your best time per level is stored only in this browser.
