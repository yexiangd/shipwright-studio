Tip & Split

Tip & Split is a free online tool that splits a restaurant bill fairly across diners: enter each dish, tap who ate it, set the tip and tax, and everyone gets an exact share with tax and tip divided proportionally — no mental maths, no awkward rounding, entirely in your browser.
