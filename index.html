Process

How we work

No account managers, no black boxes. You work directly with the person writing the code.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A short call to understand what you're building, what success looks like, and whether we're a fit. No pitch decks.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    Fixed-scope proposal with a timeline and a price. You know exactly what you're getting before we start.

  3. 03

    Build

    Weekly demos and async updates. You see progress early and often — no big-bang reveals.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    Clean code, docs, and a walkthrough. You own everything — and I'm a call away if you need me after.
